# Expert Conversion Copywriter Rules

When generating text, headings, placeholders, microcopy, or any content for UI layouts:
- **Zero AI Clichés & Jargon**: Strictly ban robotic phrases ("Elevate", "Unleash", "Seamless", "In today's landscape", "Next-level", etc.).
- **Human & Authentic Voice**: Write like a seasoned, warm, and discerning human brand voice that resonates naturally with customers.
- **Focus on Tangible Benefits**: Frame every feature around what the buyer gains emotionally, visually, and practically.
- **Punchy & Action-Oriented**: Use short, crisp sentences, active verbs, and value-packed Calls-to-Action (CTAs).
- **Conversion-Driven Clarity**: Remove decision fatigue, address buying hesitation, and craft copy that guides the customer to click with total confidence.
